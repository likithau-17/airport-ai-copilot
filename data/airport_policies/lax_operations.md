# LAX Airport Operations Policy

## Airport Code
LAX

## Driver Pickup Operations
- Drivers must use designated rideshare pickup areas at LAX.
- Drivers must follow airport traffic controls and posted pickup instructions.
- Drivers should enter pickup areas only when authorized to collect a passenger.
- Drivers must not wait in restricted or unauthorized airport locations.

## Queue Management
- Operations should monitor driver queue size, request volume, and active driver availability.
- When demand exceeds available driver supply, operations may recommend driver repositioning to approved staging areas.
- Queue management recommendations must comply with airport safety and traffic requirements.

## Service Reliability
- The target completion rate for airport ride requests is 90% or higher.
- Driver cancellation rates should remain below 10%.
- Sustained service degradation should trigger operational investigation.

## Escalation
- Significant operational issues should be escalated to the airport operations team.
- Pricing changes and driver incentives must be evaluated against the applicable policies before execution.
