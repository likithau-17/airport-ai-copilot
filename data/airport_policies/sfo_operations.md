# SFO Airport Operations Policy

## Airport Code
SFO

## Driver Pickup Operations
- Drivers must use designated rideshare pickup zones.
- Drivers should proceed to the pickup zone only after receiving a confirmed passenger request.
- Drivers must follow airport traffic officers and posted pickup instructions.
- Drivers should not wait for passenger requests in restricted airport areas.

## Queue Management
- The operations team monitors driver queue size and passenger request volume.
- When queue size becomes high, operations may recommend repositioning drivers to approved staging areas.
- Operational recommendations must comply with airport traffic and safety rules.

## Service Reliability
- The target completion rate for airport ride requests is 90% or higher.
- Driver cancellation rates should remain below 10%.
- Persistent service degradation should be investigated using current airport metrics.

## Escalation
- Significant operational issues should be escalated to the airport operations team.
- Any action that changes pricing or provides financial incentives must follow the applicable pricing and driver policies.
