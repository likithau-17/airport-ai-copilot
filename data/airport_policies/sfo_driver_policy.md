# SFO Driver Incentive Policy

## Airport Code
SFO

## Driver Incentives
- Driver incentives may be used to improve driver availability during periods of high passenger demand.
- Incentives must be based on current operational metrics.
- The assistant must verify applicable policy before calculating or recommending an incentive.

## Incentive Limits
- Incentives up to $25 per eligible driver are considered medium-impact actions.
- Incentives above $25 per eligible driver require explicit human approval.
- The assistant must not execute an incentive above the approved threshold without human approval.

## Eligibility
- Drivers must be active and eligible under the applicable driver program.
- Incentive calculations must clearly state the proposed amount and eligibility criteria.
- All incentive actions must be recorded in the audit trail.

## Compliance
- Incentives must not violate airport rules, driver agreements, or applicable company policies.
- If policy information is unavailable or conflicting, the action must be escalated for human review.
